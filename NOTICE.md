# Data Notice

This repository contains Markdown summaries of publicly disclosed 2025 annual reports of listed companies, collected from CNINFO (巨潮资讯).

- The copyright and related rights in the original annual reports belong to the respective companies and/or the original disclosure platform.
- The Markdown files are provided for research and benchmarking purposes only.
- No internal company documents, personal identity numbers, bank account numbers, or private contact details are included.
- Shareholder names, executive names, company phone numbers, and company email addresses that appear in the source annual reports are public disclosures.
- If you reuse the corpus, verify the terms of the original disclosure platform and the respective companies.

If you are a rights holder and believe that a file should be removed, please open an issue in this repository.
